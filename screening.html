EDUCATIONAL AI DEMO

Breast imaging screening demo

Upload a breast ultrasound image (PNG or JPG) and see what a trained AI model makes of it. The analysis runs entirely in your browser: your image is never uploaded or stored.

This is not a diagnosis. It is a research and educational demonstration, not a medical device. A “not suspicious” result does not rule out cancer, and a “suspicious” result does not mean you have it. If you have symptoms or questions about screening, please see a doctor.
The screening model has not been published yet. Please check back soon.

The model only works on the kind of image it was trained on. Other image types, or low-quality images, give meaningless scores. Accuracy figures come from a limited test set and will be lower on images from other hospitals or scanners.
